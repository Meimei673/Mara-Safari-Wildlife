About the destination

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Named for the Maasai people who have herded cattle across these plains for generations, and for the Mara River that cuts through it, the reserve sits in southwestern Kenya, bordering Tanzania's Serengeti. It is one of the most reliable places on Earth to see the "Big Five" — lion, leopard, elephant, rhino and buffalo — and from July to October it hosts the Great Migration, when more than a million wildebeest and zebra cross the Mara River in search of grass.

Location Narok County, southwestern Kenya, ~270 km from Nairobi
Best time to visit July – October for the migration; year-round game viewing
Nearest airstrips Keekorok, Musiara & Ol Kiombo, reached by short scheduled flights from Nairobi
Getting there by road Roughly 5–6 hours' drive from Nairobi via Narok town
